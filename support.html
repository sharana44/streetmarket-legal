Privacy Policy · Terms of Service

StreetMarket lets you order ahead from local shops — groceries, street food, hardware and more — and collect when your order is ready. SM Partner is the companion app shops use to manage their listings and orders.

Need help with something?
Email sharanaforu@gmail.com.

We reply within 2 working days. Please include the email address on your account and, for an order question, roughly when you placed it — that is usually enough for us to find it.

Reports of offensive content are the exception: we review those within 24 hours.

Common questions

How do I pay?

You pay the shop directly when you collect your order. StreetMarket does not take payment in the app and never asks for card or bank details.

My order has not been accepted

Shops accept orders manually, so there can be a short wait — and a shop that has closed for the day may not accept at all. You will get a notification the moment the status changes. If an order sits unaccepted, contact the shop or email us and we will follow it up.

I did not get my verification email

Check the spam folder first — verification mail is the message most often filtered. If it is not there, email us from the address you registered with and we will verify the account manually.

I did not get my OTP

OTP codes are sent by SMS and expire after a few minutes; each code works once, so if you have requested several, use the newest. If nothing arrives, check that the number on your account is correct and that the phone has signal, then request a fresh code.

Why does the app ask for my location?

Only to show shops near you and sort them by distance. You can decline and still browse and order — you will just need to find shops yourself. The app never tracks your location in the background.

Why does the app ask for my contacts?

Only when you choose to share a cart, so you can pick who to share it with. Your contacts are read on the device and are never uploaded to our servers.

How do I delete my account?

Open Profile in the app and choose Delete Account. You can also email us and we will action it within 30 days. See the Privacy Policy for what deletion does and does not remove.

I run a shop and want to list on StreetMarket

Download SM Partner and register your shop. New partners are reviewed before going live; email us if your approval seems to be taking too long.

Reporting offensive content or blocking someone

Shop reviews and shared-cart messages are written by other people using StreetMarket. If you see something abusive, hateful, misleading or otherwise inappropriate, you can report it from inside the app — and you can block the person who posted it.

We review every report within 24 hours and remove content that breaks our rules. Accounts that repeatedly post such content are suspended.

How to report content

On a shop review, tap the … button on the review and choose a reason. On a message in a shared cart, tap Report underneath the message. Reports are confidential — the person you report is never told who reported them.

How to block someone

The report screen has an Also block this person option. Blocking hides that person's reviews and messages from you straight away. It is one-directional and silent: they are not told, and they can still use the app.

How to unblock someone

Open Profile → Blocked users in the app. Everyone you have blocked is listed there, and Unblock restores their content for you.

Reporting without the app

You can also email sharanaforu@gmail.com with a screenshot and the shop or cart involved. Email reports are held to the same 24-hour review. If you believe someone is in danger, contact your local emergency services first.

Reporting a problem with the app

If something is broken, email us with your device model, the app version (shown at the bottom of the Profile screen) and what you were doing at the time. Screenshots help.
