Privacy Policy — StreetMarket

Effective date: 1 August 2026

StreetMarket ("we", "our") operates the StreetMarket and StreetMarket Business mobile applications, which let customers discover local shops and order food and goods for takeaway, and let shop partners manage their listings and orders.

Information we collect

  • Account information — name, email address, phone number, and password (stored as a salted hash) when you register.
  • Order information — items ordered, order history, and the shops you interact with.
  • Location — approximate or precise device location, only when you grant permission, used to show nearby shops and (for partners) to set a shop's location on the map.
  • Push notification tokens — a device token used to send you order status notifications, if you allow notifications.
  • Shop information (partners) — shop name, address, menu items, prices, and photos you upload.

How we use information

We use this information to operate the service: showing nearby shops, processing and fulfilling orders, notifying you about order status, and maintaining your account. We do not sell your personal information to third parties, and we do not use it for third-party advertising.

Payments

Orders are paid for on delivery or collection. The apps do not collect or store card or bank details.

Data sharing

Order details are shared between the customer and the shop fulfilling the order. We do not sell your personal information. We use the following service providers, each receiving only the minimum data needed to perform its function:

  • Resend — delivers account verification emails; receives your email address.
  • Expo — delivers push notifications; receives your device push token.
  • Mappls (MapmyIndia) — displays maps and shop locations.
  • Cloudinary — hosts shop and menu images uploaded by partners.
  • MongoDB Atlas and Amazon Web Services — host our database and servers.

Data security

All traffic between the apps and our servers is encrypted in transit using HTTPS/TLS. Passwords are never stored in readable form — they are kept only as a salted hash — and verification codes are likewise stored hashed and expire after a short period. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your information.

Data retention and deletion

Account data is retained while your account is active. You may request deletion of your account and associated data by emailing sharanaforu@gmail.com; we will action requests within 30 days.

Children

The apps are not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

We may update this policy; material changes will be announced in-app. The latest version is always available at this URL.
